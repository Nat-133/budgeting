PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This budgeting dashboard is a personal, self-hosted application. It is run by
one individual solely to view and analyse their own bank accounts. It is not
offered as a service to anyone else.

What data is accessed
  With the account holder's explicit consent, the application reads account
  information through a regulated Open Banking provider: account details,
  balances and transactions. It has read-only access and cannot make payments
  or change anything at the bank.

How data is used
  The data is used only to display balances, track spending against a budget
  and forecast future balances for the account holder.

Where data is stored
  Data is stored locally on the account holder's own machine. It is not
  uploaded to any third-party server operated by this application.

Sharing
  Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising,
  profiling or analytics.

Retention
  Data is kept only for as long as the account holder chooses to keep it and
  can be deleted at any time.

Withdrawing consent
  Access can be revoked at any time through the Open Banking provider or
  directly with the bank. Access also expires automatically when the consent
  period ends.

Contact
  Questions can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/Nat-133/budgeting
