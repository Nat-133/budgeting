TERMS OF USE

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This budgeting dashboard is a personal, self-hosted application used by its
author to view and analyse their own bank accounts. It is not a commercial
product and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

Use
  The application accesses account information only with the account
  holder's explicit consent, given through a regulated Open Banking provider.
  Access is read-only: the application cannot initiate payments or modify
  accounts.

No financial advice
  Figures, budgets and forecasts shown by the application are for personal
  reference only and are not financial advice.

No warranty
  The source code is published as-is, without warranty of any kind. Anyone
  who runs their own copy does so at their own risk and is responsible for
  their own data and any provider agreements.

Ending access
  Consent can be withdrawn at any time through the Open Banking provider or
  the bank, after which the application can no longer access the account.

Changes
  These terms may be updated; the current version is always the one in this
  repository.

Contact
  Questions can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/Nat-133/budgeting
